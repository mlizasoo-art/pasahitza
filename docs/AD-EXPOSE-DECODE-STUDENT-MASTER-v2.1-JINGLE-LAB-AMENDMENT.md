# AD EXPOSE · DECODE · Student Master v2.1 — Jingle Lab Amendment

**Status:** CANONICAL CONTENT/SOURCE AMENDMENT  
**Date:** 2026-08-22  
**Authority amended:** `AD-EXPOSE-DECODE-Student-Master-Content-v2.1.docx`  
**Scope:** Digital Content Appendix · `D-S5-W01 · JINGLE LAB` only.

## Reason for amendment

The source audit tightened the teaching distinction between **music used in an advertisement** and a **jingle**. For this activity, a jingle is treated as a short, simple, memorable advertising tune or sung phrase associated directly with the brand/product/service.

- The bound Mars Bar `Saxophonist` source was not a sufficiently clear jingle exemplar for that distinction: the commercial uses prominent music and the Mars slogan, but the selected source does not make the short sung advertising phrase the learner's primary evidence.
- Jacob's Club is historically defensible as a genuine advertising jingle. It is nevertheless replaced here because the execution is longer and more song-like than the clean, compact musical-phrase model the lesson is intended to establish for DBH3 learners.
- Calgon and Autoglass are retained as clearer UK exemplars: each centres a short, immediately recognisable branded musical phrase.

This is a **source/content correction**, not a redesign of the activity construct.

## Replacement canonical block

### D-S5-W01 · JINGLE LAB

**Purpose:** listening for gist, product/service type and memorability. Four short classic UK advertising clips; no lyric transcription.

1. **Calgon** — UK `Washing machines live longer with Calgon` campaign.
2. **Autoglass** — UK `Autoglass Repair, Autoglass Replace` campaign.
3. **Chicken Tonight** — UK `I feel like Chicken Tonight` campaign.
4. **WeBuyAnyCar.com** — UK dot-com jingle campaign.

### EOI-style Task 1 — Multiple Matching

Listen to clips 1–4. Match each clip to a product/service type. There are **TWO extra options**.

A. washing-machine care / water-softener product  
B. windscreen repair and replacement service  
C. cooking sauce  
D. online car-buying service  
E. sportswear  
F. mobile phone

1. ____  2. ____  3. ____  4. ____

**Key:** 1=A · 2=B · 3=C · 4=D.

### EOI-style Task 2 — Multiple Choice

**Clip 1:** What makes the jingle easy to remember?  
A. A long explanation  
B. A short sung slogan with a brand-name payoff  
C. Facts and statistics

**Clip 2:** What is the main memory device?  
A. A short musical service phrase that repeats the brand name  
B. A celebrity interview  
C. A scientific test

**Clip 3:** What helps the campaign stay in your memory?  
A. A quiet product description  
B. Repetition, rhythm and movement  
C. A price table

**Clip 4:** What is repeated most strongly?  
A. The company name  
B. A health warning  
C. A product comparison

**Key:** 1=B · 2=A · 3=B · 4=A.

### Final analyst choice — not graded

Which jingle is easiest for you to remember? Complete: `Clip ___ is the most memorable because…`

**Playback rule:** first listen without pausing; second listen may be replayed once per clip. No on-screen lyrics.
