# AD EXPOSE · Mission Briefing transcript policy v1

State: ACTIVE
Date: 2026-08-22

Applies to all four AD EXPOSE Mission Briefings: RECRUIT, DECODE, EXPOSE and CREATE.

## Learner flow

1. The Mission Briefing audio never autoplays.
2. The learner must complete two full listens before the transcript unlocks.
3. Partial playback does not count as a completed listen. A listen must begin at the start and reach the natural end without skipping forward.
4. Progress is stored locally in the learner browser so the transcript remains unlocked on later visits from the same browser profile.
5. After two complete listens, the learner can open the transcript.
6. The unlocked transcript includes a second audio player so the learner can listen again while reading the transcript.
7. The learner can hide and reopen the transcript at any time after it has been unlocked.
8. The transcript must match the actual spoken script of the published Mission Briefing; it may not be paraphrased independently from the audio.

## Protection

- Director Webb remains a locked reuse-only MP3 and is not regenerated by this feature.
- This policy changes playback/transcript behaviour only; it does not alter Mission Briefing audio bytes.
- Listening Practice is outside this change and keeps its existing assets and behaviour until separately reviewed.
